The Artist

Hi, I'm Samrat.

I started photographing weddings because I was fascinated by how much emotion happens in a single day. The loud, chaotic joy of a Baraat, the quiet nerves before walking down the aisle, the tears parents try to hide.

My philosophy is simple: you shouldn't feel like you're on a film set. You should enjoy your wedding. My team and I blend into the background so we can capture the real, unscripted moments.

Luxury isn't about holding perfect poses. It's about being entirely present in your own memories.

Samrat Rudra

Clarity & Transparency

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does wedding photography cost?+
Our custom wedding collections start from ₹1,25,000. Every wedding is unique. Once you fill out the inquiry form, we schedule a quick call to understand your events and timeline, and then provide a tailored, transparent quote with no hidden fees.
How early should we book our dates?+
To maintain our premium editing standards, we strictly limit our bookings to 8 weddings per month. We highly recommend securing your date 6 to 12 months in advance.
We are awkward in front of the camera. Is that okay?+
90% of our couples tell us this! That is precisely why our documentary approach works so well. We will never ask you to hold stiff, uncomfortable poses. We give gentle direction and then let you be yourselves.
What is your delivery timeline?+
You will receive a curated preview gallery of 30-50 highlights within 1 week of your wedding. The complete edited photo gallery takes 4-6 weeks, and cinematic films are delivered in 8-12 weeks.
